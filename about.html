About University of Petra

University of Petra is located in western Amman and hosts 7,000 undergraduate and master's students in Arts and Sciences, Administrative and Financial Sciences, Pharmacy and Medical Sciences, Information Technology, Architecture and Design, Law, Mass Communication, Engineering, and Dentistry.

Vision

University of Petra aspires to be the best choice for scholars and learners in Jordan and the region.

Mission

To play a vital role in nation-building by creating and disseminating knowledge and technology, and preparing graduates capable of contributing positively to their communities. The university aims to provide a scientific, cultural, and social environment for quality learning, creative thinking, innovation, and research.

Quality and campus

Official materials state that the university has fulfilled ISO 9001 requirements in higher-education support services and obtained quality assurance certification from the Higher Education Accreditation Commission. The university also states that it ranked first in the QS ranking of private Jordanian universities. The campus is described as a smart campus with e-learning facilities, an electronic library, and high-speed internet.
