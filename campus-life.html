Deanship of Student Affairs

More than just classes

University of Petra describes campus life as a climate of community, wellbeing, and responsible freedom. About 41% of the campus is green space, and incoming-student materials describe a multicultural community from 31 countries.

Student clubs

Clubs listed for 2025–2026 include the University of Petra Ambassadors Club, Diwan Club, Student Environment and Sustainability Club, Chemistry and Environment Club, Debate, Culture and Knowledge Club, Programming Club, Cybersecurity Club, Data Science and Artificial Intelligence Club, IEEE UOP Club, NOVA Club, Petra Verse Club, Graduate Students Club, Pharmaceutical Research Center Club, and Tube Team Club.

Sports

The Sports Activity Department oversees football, basketball, volleyball, handball, badminton, table tennis, athletics, taekwondo, kickboxing, karate, and gym use. It organizes internal championships and university teams for the Sports Federation of Jordanian Universities.

Accommodation

The on-campus female students' dormitory offers single and double rooms, study and recreation spaces, kitchens, laundry, a gym, and 24-hour educational supervisors. Housing is available year-round. Airport pickup can be arranged with two days' notice.

Semester rents published for 2023–2024 range from 750 JOD for a double room to 1,150 JOD for a penthouse apartment, plus 100 JOD transportation in a regular semester. These figures do not include living and dining costs.
