Bachelor's admission

Applicants need the Jordanian General Secondary Education Certificate or its equivalent. The high school average must meet the minimum required for the chosen major.

Required documents include a certified transcript, identity document (civil ID or passport), a personal photo, and military service book for Jordanian students. Some non-Jordanian students must provide a non-objection letter from their embassy in Jordan.

Bridging from a diploma

Jordanian applicants with a Comprehensive Community College Diploma averaging at least 68% may be admitted, except Pharmacy, Architecture, and Civil Engineering, which require at least 70%.

Up to 48 credit hours may transfer for two-year diplomas and 64 for three-year diplomas, provided no specialized course below 60% is transferred.

Master's admission

A bachelor's GPA of “Good” or equivalent is required, plus an English language test score matching the major. Master's programs offer thesis and comprehensive-exam tracks.

Thesis discussion fees are listed as 500 JOD and the comprehensive exam as 200 JOD on the official majors page (2023–2024).

Registration fees (2023–2024)

New-student fees published by the university for 2023–2024:

  • Registration: 200 JOD regular semester / 100 JOD summer
  • Service fees: 170 JOD / 85 JOD summer
  • Refundable insurance: 100 JOD
  • First-time application: 40 JOD
  • Placement tests (bachelor's): 45 JOD
